# Research Context

Use this context for investigation, comparisons, fact-finding, product evaluation, and technical research.

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources when available.
- Distinguish verified facts from inference and opinion.
- Note uncertainty, conflicting evidence, and important missing information.
- For time-sensitive subjects, verify that information is current.
- Summarize findings rather than reproducing large portions of source material.
- End with practical conclusions or next steps when the task calls for a decision.
