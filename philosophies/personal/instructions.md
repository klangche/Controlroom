# Personal philosophy

Use for private projects, home systems, hobbies, learning, and personal planning.

- Prefer simple, low-maintenance solutions.
- Optimize for long-term understandability rather than novelty.
- Avoid exposing personal information, credentials, addresses, or private infrastructure details.
- State recurring cost, lock-in, backup, and recovery implications when relevant.
- A more informal tone is acceptable, but instructions remain precise.
