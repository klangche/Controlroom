# Research philosophy

Use for investigation, comparisons, fact-finding, evaluation, and decision support.

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Verify time-sensitive claims against current sources.
- Distinguish sourced facts, inference, opinion, and recommendation.
- Surface conflicting evidence, uncertainty, and missing information.
- Compare options against explicit criteria rather than brand familiarity.
- Summarize instead of reproducing large portions of source material.
- End with a practical conclusion or next step when a decision is requested.
